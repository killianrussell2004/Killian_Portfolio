Biography

Killian Russell

MEng MECHANICAL & SUSTAINABILITY ENGINEERING — DUBLIN CITY UNIVERSITY

I'm a hands-on Mechanical & Sustainability Engineering student at Dublin City University, currently in second year with first-class academic performance — 1.1, 3rd in class. My experience spans mechanical design, CAD modelling, fabrication, hydraulics, embedded electronics, simulation, and engineering documentation.

I like taking a design the whole way: from concept, through CAD and drawings, into fabrication, fit-up, inspection, and testing. I'm just as comfortable in a workshop or out in the field as I am with a SolidWorks assembly open on screen.

Outside coursework, that same instinct shows up everywhere — currently a ground-up restoration of a 2003 Ford Ranger, years of hands-on hydraulic maintenance work, and a habit of turning ideas (like a 3D-printed wind turbine driving an Arduino-controlled water pump) into things that actually run.

I'm enthusiastic about mechanical design, manufacturing engineering, robotics, and renewable energy systems — and about solving real engineering problems from first principles rather than just from the textbook.
