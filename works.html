3D Printed Wind Turbine & Arduino-Controlled Water Pump

Wall-Mounted Helmet Holder — FMEA, SimScale & 3D Printing

Applied FMEA before finalising geometry — identified failure modes (wall pull-out, bracket fracture, surface contact damage) and used the results to drive gusset placement and fixing hole sizing. Ran FEA in SimScale to validate the bracket under worst-case helmet load, iterating geometry before committing to print in PLA.
